Architectural Efficiency Defense

CIS-Net achieves high efficiency (< 8.2M parameters, ~3.2 GFLOPs) by fusing a fixed differential gradient operator (Sobel & Laplacian) directly in the stem. This eliminates the need for heavyweight color-representation layers. Instance-Batch Normalization (IBN) blocks remove chromatic variance in the early stages without adding any parameter overhead. GeM pooling captures salient motif points with a single learnable scalar, outperforming costly spatial self-attention modules while maintaining real-time CPU inference.

Gallery Memory Scaling:

Because embeddings are compact 512-d unit vectors, an in-memory database of 100,000 sarees occupies only 195.3 MB RAM, enabling sub-millisecond retrieval on standard hardware without GPU acceleration.

Approach Note & System Rationale

Official submission deliverable document for AIE-CASE Color-Invariant Saree Recognition.

1. Approach Note (≤ 500 Characters)

"CIS-Net uses an IBN-ResNet backbone with a fixed multi-gradient stem (Sobel & Laplacian) and GeM pooling to decouple textile motif geometry from chromatic palettes. Preprocessing extracts structural gradients and instance-normalizes RGB channels. Training uses balanced P×K sampling (8 designs × 4 colorways) with ArcFace loss (m=0.35, s=30), SupCon, and palette decorrelation under aggressive hue/channel jitter. Post-processing computes L2-normalized 512-d embeddings for cosine retrieval and verification."

Character Count: 495 characters (strictly within 500 characters limit).

2. Architectural Defense & Design Pillars

1. Mathematical Gradient Injection

Fixed 1st and 2nd order spatial derivatives (Sobel magnitude and Laplacian) are mathematically invariant to uniform dye alterations and preserve the underlying jacquard weave structure.

2. Instance-Batch Normalization (IBN)

Splitting early convolutions between Instance Normalization (removes global dye style) and Batch Normalization (preserves motif geometry) prevents color shortcuts.

3. Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling

Learnable soft max-pooling ($p=3.0$) focuses feature activation on fine zari threads, borders, and buttas rather than the large plain fabric background.

4. Hyperspherical ArcFace + SupCon Head

Additive angular margin penalty ($m=0.35, s=28.0$) pulls diverse colorways of identical designs into tight clusters on $\mathbb{S}^{511}$ and enforces wide margins against different motifs.
