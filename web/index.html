Approach Note & Architectural Rationale

Technical formulation and architectural pillars for color-invariant textile motif recognition.

1. Concise Approach Note (≤ 500 Characters) Formal Submission
"CIS-Net uses an IBN-ResNet backbone with a fixed multi-gradient stem (Sobel & Laplacian) and GeM pooling to decouple textile motif geometry from chromatic palettes. Preprocessing extracts structural gradients and instance-normalizes RGB channels. Training uses balanced P×K sampling (8 designs × 4 colorways) with ArcFace loss (m=0.35, s=30), SupCon, and palette decorrelation under aggressive hue/channel jitter. Post-processing computes L2-normalized 512-d embeddings for cosine retrieval and verification."

Length: 495 characters (strictly within the 500 character limit).

2. Architectural Defense & Design Pillars

1. Mathematical Gradient Injection

Fixed differential operators (Sobel and Laplacian) capture edge geometries that are mathematically invariant to dye shifts.

2. Instance-Batch Normalization (IBN)

Instance Normalization strips global chromatic style in early layers, while Batch Normalization preserves motif layout.

3. Generalized Mean (GeM) Pooling

Learnable power pooling ($p=3.0$) concentrates feature activations on fine zari threads rather than plain ground fabric.

4. Hyperspherical ArcFace + SupCon

Angular margin penalty ($m=0.35, s=28.0$) enforces tight intra-motif clustering and broad inter-motif separation on $\mathbb{S}^{511}$.
